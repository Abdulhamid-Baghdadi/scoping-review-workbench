# UI audit, 31 October 2026

Scoping Review Workbench checked against `docs/ui-ux-rules.md` after the visual refresh of 31 October
(`docs/visual-refresh-plan.md`, steps 1–7 and 9; step 8 skipped): the fonts served from `web/fonts/`, a colour per
phase and per decision, the journey map and page header in phase colours, the screening record as a card with a
stamp for each decision, our own phase pictures and icons in place of emoji, and the new Homepage.

**How it was checked:**
- **Measured (script `tests/smoke/a11y.mjs`):** contrast, targets, names, field labels, toggle states, headings and
  sideways scrolling.
  - `npm run smoke` runs these on every page of the example, light and dark, and at 400 px, and since this refresh
    on the Homepage too.
  - This audit also ran them where the smoke test does not go: every page at **320 px**, every page with its **AI
    card open**, the **Help drawer open**, the **Settings menu open**, the **Homepage** and the **start screen**, in
    light, dark and at 320 px.
- **From the tokens (`tests/unit/style.test.js`):** every text colour on every surface at 4.5:1 and every field edge
  and focus ring at 3:1, in both themes, from `style.css` itself; the phase pictures' labels at 4.5:1 and lines at 3:1
  on their colour.
- **Motion:** with reduced motion asked for, the card fan, the stamp and the slide do not move, and the Homepage
  numbers show at once.
- **Read in the code and tried in the browser:** the pages changed, for the heuristic rules (one primary action, words,
  icons beside words, feedback).

The audit of 20 October (`docs/ui-audit-2026-10-20.md`) left one open problem (its #9, below as #7).

## What already works

- **The measured floor passes everywhere it was run:** every page, the AI cards, the Help drawer, the Settings menu,
  the Homepage and the start screen, in light, dark and at 320 px. No console errors.
- **Colour is never alone** (WCAG 1.4.1): every phase colour comes with the phase's name, every decision colour with
  ✓ ? ✕ and its word, every picture with alt text.
- **Contrast is a test, not a look:** the token test fails before a page is drawn if a colour pair drops below AA.
- **Offline:** the 11 font files are in the offline list (`/web/precache.json`) and served as `font/woff2`; no font
  is fetched from another site, and a test holds that.
- **Motion is calm and optional:** nothing loops; the stamp, the slide and the fan play once per decision or visit and
  stop under reduced motion. The next decision key never waits for an animation (the stamp shows within about
  0.17 s of the key, including the save).
- **Looks follow function**, Kurosu & Kashimura (1995): fast keyboard screening, the decision log and every count
  behave as before; the smoke test's screening, fast-key and stamp checks pass.

## Not checked

- **Screen readers:** no real screen reader was used; names, roles and live regions were checked in the DOM.
- **The README as GitHub shows it**, in light and dark: it shows only after a push.

- **One evaluator:** a heuristic evaluation by 3–5 people finds more, Nielsen & Landauer (1993).
