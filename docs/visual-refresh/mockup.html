Design for assembly and disassembly of battery packs

Collijn, Mikaela; Johansson, Emma

This thesis studies how the design of battery packs for electric vehicles affects how easily they can be taken apart for repair, reuse and recycling. It reviews joining methods, from welded busbars to bolted modules, and proposes a set of design for disassembly guidelines for pack engineers.
